# How To Read Amigurumi Patterns（All About Ami）

- 来源URL: https://www.allaboutami.com/howtoreadamigurumipatterns/
- 抓取日期: 2026-10-30
- 语言: 英文
- 主题: 英文图解读法（逐圈指令、括号针数、星号重复）

## 正文

I have been receiving e-mails recently from people asking how to read amigurumi patterns. I always assumed it was straight forward enough, but I often forget what it was like when I first started crocheting. Trying to interpret patterns can seem like trying to understand a foreign language with all the symbols and abbreviations! And so, I thought I would take the time to explicitly and clearly describe how to read amigurumi crochet patterns...

Reference the "Amigurumi for Beginners" blog post for videos on basic stitches and materials. There is a chart showing common abbreviations found in crochet patterns (see a more extensive list at purplekittyyarns.com).

Then the article walks through one of its patterns (e.g. Teddy Ornaments), explaining round by round:

- **Translation**: *Work six single crochets in a magic ring.*
  - The "R" stands for "Round" because we are crocheting in the round in a continuous spiral (could also be denoted by "Rnd"). "R" could also stand for "Row" (when working in rows, you usually turn your work at the end of each row and work back across the top of the previous row for a square or rectangle).
  - The number in brackets at the end shows how many stitches you should have completed during that round. "I always count how many stitches I've completed to double-check my work compared to the pattern to ensure I haven't skipped a stitch or accidentally done one too many."
  - Note: in earlier patterns, Round 1 often looked like: "**R1**: Ch 2, 6 sc in second ch from hook. (6 sts)" — interchangeable with the Magic Circle version.

- **Translation**: *Work two single crochets in each single crochet around.* Since you are crocheting two stitches in each stitch around, you are doubling the stitch count (from 6 to 12) so that your circle expands outwards. Patterns that say "inc in next st all round" mean the same thing.

- **Translation**: Work one single crochet in the next stitch. Then work two single crochets in the following stitch. Repeat the pattern in asterisks 6 times.（星号 `*...*` 表示重复段）

- **Translation**: Work two single crochets in the next two stitches（继续逐圈解释 sc / inc / dec、颜色更换、连缝与塞棉等环节，原文配实拍步骤图逐圈讲解）。
