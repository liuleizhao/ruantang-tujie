## 正文

Coiled teal pigtails that spring off her shoulders, and headphones arcing right over the top of her head — this doll sat in my sketchbook for almost a year. I kept putting it off because the hair felt intimidating, and the first two prototypes proved me right. Version one had pigtails so heavy they dragged her whole head sideways. Version two fixed the weight but looked stiff, like a helmet. The third pass cracked it: **each pigtail is one long strip worked three separate times into a single foundation chain, and the graduated stitch heights coil it into a spiral** the second you fasten off. The fringe gets sculpted in irregular layers straight onto a simple wig cap. No wire, no wig frame, no cursing.

She lands at about **5.5 inches (14 cm)**… She reads as a cool chibi idol doll with genuinely ridiculous hair…

**结构亮点（The clever bits）**：

- the **legs connect straight into the body round** so there's no awkward crotch seam（腿直接并入身体圈，无裆缝）
- the **skirt grows directly out of a ridge of free front loops** instead of being made separately and wrestled on（裙子从预留外半针直接长出来）
- the **head is built in two layers** — an inner base shaped from the body itself, then a larger head wrapped around the neck so it physically cannot pop off（头分两层，内层由身体成型，外层包住脖子，物理上不会脱落）
- The only real sewing is **the arms, the hair, and the accessories**（真正需要缝的只有手臂、头发、配件）。

**难度判定**：She sits at solid intermediate because of the **body-to-head wrap in Phase 3** and the **hand-sculpted fringe in Phase 5**, neither of which runs on a one-to-one stitch pattern you can follow blindly.

**材料与密度**：

- worsted-weight acrylic (Category 4) + **3mm hook**，"a tight gauge on purpose. You want stitches dense enough that the stuffing never peeks through and the head holds a clean round shape under the weight of the hair."
- acrylic 的理由：stitch definition 清晰、不起毛模糊细刘海、**重量轻**——"extra weight in the hair is exactly what tips a chibi head sideways"。
- "The 3mm hook is non-negotiable for the body pieces — size up and you get gaps at this scale, and she stops looking chibi and starts looking loose."
